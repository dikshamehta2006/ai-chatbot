# Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Coping Strategies

## 1. The Cognitive Triangle
Thoughts, emotions, and behaviors are deeply interconnected. When we encounter an adverse event:
1. An automatic thought arises ("I failed, I am worthless").
2. This triggers a visceral emotion (shame, despair, anxiety).
3. The emotion drives behavioral withdrawal or rumination.
Intervention point: By identifying and gently challenging automatic thoughts, we can soften our emotional response and choose constructive behaviors.

## 2. Common Cognitive Distortions
- **All-or-Nothing Thinking**: Viewing situations in black-and-white categories (e.g., "If I am not perfect, I am a total failure").
- **Catastrophizing**: Anticipating the worst-case scenario and assuming you will not be able to cope.
- **Mental Filter**: Dwelling exclusively on a single negative detail while ignoring positive or neutral experiences.
- **Mind Reading**: Assuming you know what others are thinking without tangible evidence ("They all secretly dislike me").
- **Emotional Reasoning**: Believing that because you feel a certain way, it must be the objective truth ("I feel overwhelmed, so everything must be falling apart").

## 3. The 3-Column Thought Restructuring Technique
When overwhelmed by a negative automatic thought:
1. **Identify the Automatic Thought**: Write down the exact thought ("Nobody cares about me").
2. **Find the Distortion**: Identify which distortion applies (e.g., All-or-Nothing, Mind Reading).
3. **Formulate a Compassionate, Balanced Alternative**: Replace with a grounded, fair statement:
   - "I feel lonely right now, but there are people in my life who care about me, even if they are busy today. Reaching out to one friend can help."

## 4. Behavioral Activation (Gentle Steps)
When motivation is low due to depression or burnout:
- Break overwhelming tasks into micro-steps (e.g., sitting up, drinking a glass of water, walking for 2 minutes).
- Celebrate micro-wins without self-criticism.
- Action often precedes motivation; do not wait to feel 100% ready.
