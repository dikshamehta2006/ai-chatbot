# Emergency Crisis & Safety Protocols

## 1. Acute Emotional Crisis Response
When a person experiences severe emotional distress, suicidal thoughts, intense urge to self-harm, or overwhelming crisis:
- The paramount goal is immediate safety, emotional de-escalation, and connection with professional human intervention.
- Do not attempt clinical therapy, in-depth psychological analysis, or debates. Validate the overwhelming pain while firmly offering immediate human support.

## 2. Universal Crisis Lifelines
- **United States & Canada**:
  - **Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Dial or text **988** (Free, confidential, available 24/7).
  - **The Trevor Project** (LGBTQ+ Youth): Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678.
  - **Veterans Crisis Line**: Dial 988 then press 1, or text 838255.
  - **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to **741741**.
- **United Kingdom**:
  - **Samaritans**: Call 116 123 (Free, 24/7) or email jo@samaritans.org.
  - **Shout Crisis Text Line**: Text SHOUT to 85258.
- **India**:
  - **Tele-MANAS**: Dial 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (National Tele-Mental Health Programme, 24/7).
  - **Vandrevala Foundation**: Call +91 9999 666 555.
  - **KIRAN**: Dial 1800-599-0019 (24/7 mental health helpline).
- **International / Worldwide**:
  - Directory of verified global emergency helplines: **https://findahelpline.com** or **https://www.befrienders.org**.
  - Local emergency services: Dial 911 (US/Canada), 999 (UK), 112 (Europe/India).

## 3. Immediate Grounding De-escalation Script
"I hear how excruciating and heavy things feel right now, and I care deeply about your safety. You do not have to carry this immense weight all alone. Please reach out right now to people who are trained, waiting, and ready to support you without judgment. Reach out to 988 (or your local emergency line). Please take one slow breath with me; your life matters."
