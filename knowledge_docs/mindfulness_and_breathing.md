# Somatic Grounding & Mindfulness Exercises

## 1. 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique
When experiencing dissociation, panic, or overwhelming anxiety, reconnect with the present physical environment through sensory anchoring:
- **5 things you can SEE**: Look around and silently identify five specific items (e.g., the texture on the wall, the grain of the wood desk, a shadow, a plant leaf).
- **4 things you can TOUCH**: Physically feel four distinct textures (e.g., the fabric of your sleeves, the coolness of your keys, your feet on the floor, smooth glass).
- **3 things you can HEAR**: Tune into subtle external sounds (e.g., distant traffic hum, air conditioner, a clock ticking, your own steady breath).
- **2 things you can SMELL**: Notice two aromas (e.g., coffee, hand lotion, fresh air, paper).
- **1 thing you can TASTE**: Focus on a single taste (e.g., water, mint, or the lingering taste on your tongue).

## 2. Box Breathing (Navy SEAL Calming Protocol)
A simple 4-part square breathing method to stabilize heart rate variability:
1. **Inhale** slowly through the nose for 4 counts.
2. **Hold** breath with gentle stillness for 4 counts.
3. **Exhale** steadily through the mouth for 4 counts.
4. **Hold** empty lungs with ease for 4 counts.
Repeat for 4 to 6 cycles.

## 3. Physiological Sigh (Fastest Stress Reliever)
Discovered by neurobiologists, this is the body's natural reflexive breath to rebalance carbon dioxide and oxygen levels:
1. Take two consecutive inhales through the nose: one long deep inhale, followed immediately by a quick sharp top-off inhale.
2. Release with a long, slow sigh through an open mouth until all air is emptied.
3. Perform 2 to 3 sighs to immediately lower blood pressure and heart rate.

## 4. The Self-Compassion Break (Kristin Neff)
In moments of emotional pain or self-reproach:
1. Place a warm hand over your heart or on your forearm.
2. Acknowledge: "This is a moment of suffering or difficulty."
3. Normalize: "Suffering and feeling inadequate at times is part of shared human experience; I am not alone."
4. Offer kindness: "May I be gentle and patient with myself right now. May I give myself the compassion I need."
